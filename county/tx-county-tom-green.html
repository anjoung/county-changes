Tom Green County, Texas

FIPS 48451

Scans: p. 160, p. 161, p. 220

Events

  • By 1880, Bexar County (district) was abolished into this county and Dawson County and Gaines County and Crockett County and Martin County and Howard County and Mitchell County and Nolan County and Fisher County and Scurry County and Borden County: by 1880 its 1870 area was part of Tom Green, Crockett, Martin, Howard, Mitchell, Nolan, Fisher, Scurry, Borden, Dawson, and Gaines (p. 161, Note 3).
  • Between 1870 and 1880, this county was formed from Bexar County (district) (p. 161, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Coke County was formed from this county (p. 153, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Crane County was formed from this county (p. 153, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Ector County was formed from this county (p. 155, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Glasscock County was formed from this county (p. 155, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Irion County was formed from this county (p. 157, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Loving County was formed from this county (p. 157, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Midland County was formed from this county (p. 159, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Upton County was formed from this county (p. 161, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Ward County was formed from this county (p. 161, Notes column).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, Winkler County was formed from this county (p. 161, Notes column).
  • Between 1890 and 1900, Sterling County was formed from this county (p. 161, Notes column).
  • Between 1900 and 1910, Reagan County was formed from this county (p. 159, Notes column).
