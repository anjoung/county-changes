Pierce County, Minnesota

FIPS 27113.1

Scans: p. 86, p. 87, p. 205

Events

  • In 1860, Lac qui Parle County (old) was formed from this county: The 1870 census area of old Lac qui Parle County was in Pierce in 1860 (p. 87, Note 3).
  • Between 1850 and 1860, Wahnata County was abolished into this county and Stearns County and Todd County and Otter Tail County and Douglas County and Wilkin County: to Pierce; Stearns; Todd; Otter Tail; Douglas; Toombs (Wilkin) (p. 87, Note 9).
  • Between 1850 and 1860, this county was formed from Dakota County and Wahnata County (p. 87, Notes column).
  • Between 1860 and 1870, Chippewa County was formed from this county and Renville County (p. 85, Notes column).
  • Between 1860 and 1870, Big Stone County was formed from this county (p. 85, Notes column).
  • Between 1860 and 1870, this county was abolished into Big Stone County and Stevens County and Pope County and Chippewa County and Lac qui Parle County (old), and in small part Traverse County: To Big Stone; Stevens; Pope; Chippewa; Lac qui Parle (old); ; Traverse (p. 87, Note 7).
  • Between 1860 and 1870, Pope County was formed from this county (p. 87, Notes column).
  • Between 1860 and 1870, Stevens County was formed from this county (p. 87, Notes column).

Source: Richard L. Forstall (1996), Population of States and Counties of the United States: 1790 to 1990, U.S. Bureau of the Census (public domain). Always check a row against the scan it links to.
