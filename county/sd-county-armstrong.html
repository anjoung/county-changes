Armstrong County, South Dakota

FIPS 46001

Scans: p. 142, p. 143, p. 217

Events

  • After 1870, this county was formed: An earlier Armstrong County was created after 1870 (p. 146, Note 2).
  • By 1880, this county was abolished into Hutchinson County: was part of Hutchinson County by 1880 (p. 146, Note 2).
  • Between 1880 and 1890, this county was formed from Stanley County: *Stanley (p. 146, Note 2).
  • In 1890, this county was reported as Pyatt: reported in 1890 (as Pyatt) (p. 146, Note 2).
  • In 1910, Cheyenne River Reservation was abolished into Dewey County and this county and Schnasse County and Sterling County: its area was reported in 1910 in Dewey, Armstrong, Schnasse, and Sterling (p. 147, Note 13).
  • Between 1910 and 1920, Ziebach County was formed from this county and Schnasse County and Sterling County (p. 145, Notes column).
  • In 1920, this county had its population reported with Ziebach County: enumerated with Ziebach in 1920 (p. 146, Note 2).
  • In 1954, this county was abolished into Dewey County: annexed to Dewey in 1954 (p. 146, Note 2).
  • 1890 and 1900, this county had its population reported with Cheyenne River Reservation: reported in 1890 (as Pyatt) and 1900 although largely included with Cheyenne River Indian Reservation (p. 146, Note 2).

Source: Richard L. Forstall (1996), Population of States and Counties of the United States: 1790 to 1990, U.S. Bureau of the Census (public domain). Always check a row against the scan it links to.
