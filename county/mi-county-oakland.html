Oakland County, Michigan

FIPS 26125

Scans: p. 82, p. 83, p. 204

Events

  • In 1820, this county is noted: The 1820 census boundaries of Oakland County did not include any of present-day Oakland County. (p. 83, Note 12).
  • Between 1810 and 1820, this county was formed from Mackinac County (p. 83, Notes column).
  • Between 1820 and 1830, St. Clair County was formed from Macomb County and this county (p. 83, Notes column).
  • Between 1820 and 1830, St. Joseph County was formed from this county (p. 83, Notes column).
  • Between 1830 and 1840, Genesee County was formed from this county (p. 81, Notes column).
  • Between 1830 and 1840, Lapeer County was formed from this county (p. 81, Notes column).
  • Between 1830 and 1840, Livingston County was formed from this county and Washtenaw County (p. 81, Notes column).
  • Between 1830 and 1840, Saginaw County was formed from this county and St. Clair County and St. Joseph County: *Oakland, St. Clair, St. Joseph (p. 83, Note 13).
  • Between 1830 and 1840, Shiawassee County was formed from this county (p. 83, Notes column).
